# Community code of conduct / 社区行为准则

This project welcomes contributors across languages, regions, backgrounds and scholarly interpretations.

- Discuss **claims, sources and methods**, not identities.
- Critique ideas rigorously; avoid harassment, personal attacks, intimidation and discriminatory language.
- Do not disclose private information or credentials.
- Historical texts may include offensive period terminology; discuss these critically and in context, never as an invitation to target people today.
- Do not use the community as a venue for targeted political persuasion or coordinated harassment.
- Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect contributors.

To report abuse, prefer GitHub's report/block mechanisms or contact the maintainer via a private GitHub channel where available. Do not post identifying information in a public issue.
